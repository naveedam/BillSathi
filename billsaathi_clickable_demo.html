Frequently asked questions

Is the projected bill guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate that can change as treatment and charges change.

Does pre-authorisation guarantee claim payment?

No. The insurer assesses the final claim under the policy terms.

What if I disagree with a charge?

Ask the hospital billing desk for the itemised charge and clarification.
